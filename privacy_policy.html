StrideLife Privacy Policy

App: StrideLife: Step Counter & Run (package com.zenviroxtech.stridelife)
Developer: Zenvirox Tech — Allied Outsource Technology Services (SMC-Private) Limited
Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal and health data the StrideLife app accesses, collects and uses, why, where it is stored, whether it is shared, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. StrideLife is a general fitness and wellness app. It is not a medical device.

Summary: StrideLife accesses and collects health and fitness data only to show it to you inside the app. All data is stored on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties or use it for advertising. StrideLife has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs and does not use the advertising ID.

1. Health data we access and collect

StrideLife accesses and collects the following health and fitness data. Each type is used only for the purpose listed.

2. Other data

  • Profile you enter: name (optional), gender, birth year, height and weight — used only to calculate stride length, calories and BMI.
  • Bluetooth wearables: if you pair a watch or band, StrideLife connects over Bluetooth LE to read heart rate and battery level, set the time and forward call and app notifications that you choose. Notification content is sent only to your own paired device and is not stored.
  • App Lock: your PIN is stored on the device only as a one-way hash. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; StrideLife never receives biometric data.
  • Purchase / licence check: Google Play confirms that you bought the app. We do not receive your payment details.

3. Health Connect

StrideLife reads and writes Health Connect data only after you grant permission on the Health Connect screen, and only for the types and purposes in section 1. You can see and change these permissions at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → StrideLife.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Health Connect data is used only to provide and improve the features you see in StrideLife.
  • It is never transferred to third parties, sold, or used for advertising, credit-worthiness or any purpose unrelated to the app's fitness features.
  • No person reads your Health Connect data.

4. Where your data is stored

All health, fitness and profile data is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. StrideLife does not have its own servers and does not upload your data anywhere.

If you choose Back up data, the app creates a backup file and saves it only to the place you pick (for example your Google Drive or a folder on your phone). That file stays under your control; we never receive it.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent your health data or personal data to anyone. Data leaves the app only when you choose to share it — for example when you share a workout card, a Monthly Wrapped image, export a GPX or CSV file, or save a backup — and only to the app or place you select.

6. Security

Data is kept in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can protect the app with App Lock (PIN or fingerprint). Data you share or export is protected by the app or service you send it to.

7. How long we keep data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes all of it from the device. Data written to Health Connect stays in Health Connect until you delete it there.

8. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Me → Settings → Data management → Delete all data.
  • Health Connect data: Android Settings → Health Connect → Data and access.
  • Or simply uninstall StrideLife.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. If you have questions, contact us below.

9. Children

StrideLife is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
